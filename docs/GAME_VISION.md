# Game Vision — Thistle & Crown

## Pitch

**Thistle & Crown** is a stylized medieval fantasy MOBA set in a contested highland vale. Two factions fight for influence over an ancient monolith known as the **Heartstone**.

The game aims for classic 5v5 MOBA structure with a distinct Scottish/British-inspired visual identity: comic-3D silhouettes, painted surfaces, mist, pines, stone circles, and dramatic keeps.

## The Heartstone

At the center of the highlands stands a weathered monolith called the Heartstone. It is not a simple relic. Local lore claims it shapes:

- forest growth
- weather
- animal migrations
- the thin place between living and spirit
- soil fertility
- the potency of highland magic

No faction fully understands it. Both believe they cannot afford to lose it.

## Factions

### The Highland Covenant

Clan-bound highlanders, wardens, druids, and oathbound warriors. They treat the Heartstone as the living heart of their land. Their warbands fight with improvisation, loyalty, and wild nature magic. They are not saints — raids, blood feuds, and hard bargains stain their history.

**Tone:** rugged, emotional, nature-bound, defiant.

### The Iron Crown

A disciplined royal army of knights, longbow companies, engineers, and court mages. They see the Heartstone as a strategic power source that could stabilize a fracturing realm. Their banners promise order — and demand obedience. They are not cartoon villains; many believe they are preventing a greater collapse.

**Tone:** organized, technical, authoritarian, resolute.

Neither side is simply good or evil. Both have understandable aims and moral grey zones.

## The Battlefield

Matches take place in a highland valley of old roads, ruined keeps, marshy midground, pine woods, and standing stones.

- Highland main structure: **Clanheart**
- Crown main structure: **Royal Bastion**

Destroying the enemy main structure ends the match.

## Design Pillars

1. Readable MOBA combat from a raised camera
2. Distinct faction fantasy without copying protected IPs
3. Data-driven balance and content
4. Comic-3D identity over realism
5. Architecture ready for authoritative multiplayer later
