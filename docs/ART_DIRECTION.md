# Art Direction

## Identity

Comic-3D highland warfare: bold silhouettes, painted materials, readable team colors, and atmospheric Scottish landscape cues. Inspired in *mood* by rugged medieval comic games, never a copy of any existing title’s characters, UI, or branding.

## Characters

- Overstated body language and weapon scale
- Large hands / clear gear shapes
- Strong silhouettes from MOBA camera distance
- Few tiny details; prefer broad cloth and metal planes
- Asymmetric armor plates and clan cloth
- Hand-painted texture feel (brush, wear, dye)
- No photorealism, no generic plastic mobile look, no anime proportions

## Environment

- Twisted pines, wet grass, dark earth
- Heather, mist bands, marsh midground
- Standing stones, ruined walls, timber barricades
- Readable lane ribbons through the vale
- Dramatic keeps for Clanheart and Royal Bastion

## Palette

- Highland: moor green, muted teal-blue, warm cloth gold
- Crown: desaturated crimson, iron grey, royal banner ochre
- Shared: parchment cream, anthracite, weathered wood

## Rendering targets (prototype → polish)

1. Directional key light + soft fill
2. Atmospheric fog
3. Contact / blob shadows under units
4. Selection ring and ability ground decals
5. Later: light cel shading, rim light, subtle color grade

Performance beats exotic shaders. Placeholders are procedural meshes until Meshy GLBs arrive via the asset manifest.
